# Little Peanut Support

Little Peanut is a baby tracker for iPhone: feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping and tummy time,
shared between the caregivers of one household through iCloud. This page answers the
questions that come up most. For anything else, write to ronnie.simonelli@gmail.com; say which iPhone
and iOS version you're on and, if it helps, what you did just before.

## Getting started

**Do I need an account?** No. Little Peanut uses the iCloud account already signed in on
your iPhone. There is nothing to sign up for and no password.

**The app says "Sign in to iCloud" or "Can't Reach iCloud".** The household lives in iCloud,
so the app needs an iCloud account to create or join one. Sign in under Settings ▸ your
name, make sure iCloud Drive is on, then open the app again; it keeps trying on its own.
"Can't Reach iCloud" after a restart usually clears itself once the phone is unlocked and
online.

**Setting up:** on the welcome screen, **Set Up My Family** creates the household, with
your baby's name and birthday and your own name. One person does this; everyone else joins.

## Sharing with a partner

**Inviting:** Settings ▸ Caregivers ▸ **Invite with a QR Code** shows a code the other
caregiver scans with their camera, or **Invite or Manage Caregivers** opens the iCloud
sharing sheet to send a link by Messages or Mail. The invitation is an iCloud share, so
both phones need to be signed in to iCloud.

**Joining:** open the link or scan the code on the other phone and tap **Accept & Join**,
then add your name. From then on both phones see the same entries, and every entry shows
who logged it.

**Managing:** the sharing sheet under Invite or Manage Caregivers also removes a caregiver
or stops sharing altogether.

## Timers and the Lock Screen

A running feed, sleep or pump shows as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the
Dynamic Island on every caregiver's phone, with buttons to end it or switch sides. The
other phones' timers update within seconds while they're online; if a push doesn't get
through, they catch up through iCloud a little later. An optional activity shows how long
the baby has been awake and when the next sleep is likely (Settings ▸ Predictions).

If the Lock Screen timer doesn't appear, check Settings ▸ Little Peanut ▸ **Live
Activities** is on, and that Settings ▸ Face ID & Passcode ▸ Live Activities is allowed on
the Lock Screen.

## Siri

Say "Start a bottle in Little Peanut", "Start a nap in Little Peanut" or "Wake up in Little
Peanut" to log without touching the phone. Siri asks for what the phrase left out (how much,
or when a bottle began). Help ▸ Siri in the app lists every phrase.

## Reading the day

Today shows the day as rings and a timeline. Swipe left to read any past day the same way.
Insights has the week at a glance, Daily Patterns (every day side by side, wake to wake)
and the reports; charts can be played as audio graphs with VoiceOver.

## Night mode and themes

Night mode dims the app to a warm, low-contrast look for night feeds, on a schedule or
whenever you choose (Settings ▸ Night Mode). Themes for the day and the night are picked
per iPhone from Settings or by holding the Today heading.

## Exporting, backing up and restoring

- **Export History** (Settings) writes every entry between two days as a CSV for Numbers,
  Sheets or Excel.
- **Share Summary** in Insights makes a PDF for the pediatrician.
- **Back Up Data** (Settings) saves the whole household, photos included, to a file you
  keep. On a new iPhone, **Restore from a Backup** on the welcome screen brings it back.
  Restoring works on a phone that has no household yet.

## Privacy

There is no account, no analytics and no advertising; the household lives in your iCloud,
and the developer keeps no copy. The full policy: https://ronniesimonelli.github.io/littlepeanut/privacy/.
